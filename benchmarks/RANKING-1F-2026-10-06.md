## Context

Item 1d moved the no-good-match gate to read the best raw cosine that survives the Stage 1
filter. The 0.30 threshold had been tuned on unfiltered hits, and on the gated arm MRR@5
(ambiguous) fell 0.6124 to 0.5663 with overlapping intervals, so 1f owed a retune decision. The
2026-08-05 triage left 35 misses, of which 21 expect mandatory rules (always-on channel, never
ranked by design) and 3 expect routed process rules; it asked for a metric-definition decision
before splitting the benchmark by channel. Two sibling rules collided on a generic word: Q193
expects CLEAN-DEAD-001 and lost it to CLEAN-RETURN-001 on "return". A cosine/max-cosine vector
normalization was measured as a candidate ranking change.

## Decisions

### 1. Keep the abstention threshold at 0.30

Sweep on the gated arm, 193 gold queries, false injection (FI) over 20 negatives with 95% Wilson
intervals, all from one ungated run replayed through each threshold. "n/r" marks a cell not
quoted in the decision data; the regenerated sweep file has every cell.

Every flavor-A (off-domain) negative scores at or below 0.227, so any threshold above 0.23 already
rejects all of them. The four flavor-B (coding-adjacent) negatives score 0.361 to 0.455 and sit
above 0.30; rejecting them needs a threshold near 0.46, which loses more than 22 gold queries. The
lowest gold signals are Q66 0.275, Q175 0.285 and Q58 0.311.

Decision: keep 0.30. It is the knee: below it false injection climbs (0.25 at 0.28, 0.40 at
0.24 to 0.26, 0.50 at 0.20) to recover at most two gold queries; above it hit@5 falls quickly (9
lost at 0.34). No move clears the program's interval rule: hit@5 at 0.30 overlaps every
neighbour, and with 20 negatives the FI intervals overlap across the whole 0.20 to 0.40 range.

Alternatives considered: 0.28 (recovers Q66 at FI 0.25, interval [0.11, 0.47], overlapping 0.30's);
0.20 to 0.26 (no recall gain over 0.28 that the gold set shows, FI 0.40 to 0.50); 0.32 to 0.40
(lower FI, but loses Q58 at 0.32 and 9 to 22 queries above). Trade-off accepted: four near-domain
negatives keep injecting. Precondition for any future change: expand the negatives to about 100,
weighted toward near-domain queries, so the FI interval can separate thresholds.

Measured outcome after the channel split: in `THRESHOLD-SWEEP-2026-10-06.md` (regenerated on
this date from the pre-rewording corpus, which reproduces every all-pool number above), the
eligible pool (n=169) at 0.30 scores hit@5 0.9172 [0.8757, 0.9586], MRR@5 ambiguous 0.5663
[0.4447, 0.6851] and nDCG@10 0.8254 [0.7818, 0.8673], with false injection 4/20 = 0.2000
[0.0807, 0.4160] and Q66 and Q175 lost against the ungated run. The eligible pool at 0.00 scores
hit@5 0.9290 [0.8876, 0.9645], so the 0.30 gate costs the same two queries in both pools and the
decision does not change under the new definition.

### 2. Report ranked-eligible targets separately from always-on and routed targets

`scripts/measure_retrieval.py` now scores rank metrics over the ranked-eligible pool (n=169) and
scores the always-on (21) and routed (3) targets as delivery checks. No check was rewritten for it:
the channel partition and the routed orphan and empty-route checks moved out of
`benchmarks/bench_targets.py` into `tests/fixtures/retrieval_scoring.py`, the mandatory-id read into
`writ/graph/integrity/frequency_checks.py::mandatory_rule_ids`, and always-on delivery is
`detect_stranded_mandatory`; the benchmark and the script call the same code. This is a definitional
change: the headline number moves because the denominator changes, not because retrieval changed.
Every output says so, and the pre-split all-queries pool is still reported, labelled, for continuity.
The script also gained a threshold-sweep mode (one ungated run replayed through each threshold,
checked against the live gate at the served threshold), which supersedes
`scripts/measure_false_injection.py`'s raw-cosine sweep for choosing thresholds, and a paired
bootstrap of per-query differences in `compare`, reported beside the unchanged disjoint-interval
verdict. Alternatives considered: replacing the all-queries pool outright (loses comparability with
every earlier record); counting always-on targets as hits (hides a stranded mandatory rule instead of
checking for one).

### 3. Reword CLEAN-DEAD-001 and CLEAN-RETURN-001

CLEAN-DEAD-001 gains the vocabulary its subject uses: statements after a return, raise, break or
continue that can never run, unused imports, unused variables. CLEAN-RETURN-001's trigger no longer
claims the generic word "return"; it describes branch-dependent result types and sentinel failure
values. The text changed in `writ-corpus.cypher` and identically in
`scripts/seed_phase_2a_clean_dry.py`; examples, enforcement and rationale did not. The wording was
evaluated on the same gold set that motivated it (Q193 and Q189), so any gain is in-sample.

Measured outcome: `CLEAN-REWORD-2026-10-06.md` compares two runs with different corpus digests
(a2420df5008f before, 30fd101d039f after) and identical channels (169, 21, 3) and delivery (nothing
undelivered, no orphans, no channelless targets). Every one of the twelve arm, pool and metric rows
is no decision; none regressed. On the gated arm, eligible pool: hit@5 0.9172 [0.8757, 0.9586] to
0.9290 [0.8876, 0.9645], MRR@5 ambiguous 0.5663 [0.4447, 0.6851] to 0.5982 [0.4794, 0.7135],
nDCG@10 0.8254 [0.7818, 0.8673] to 0.8310 [0.7879, 0.8720], each 2 wins and 0 losses (sign test
p=0.5000). On the ungated arm, eligible pool: hit@5 0.9290 [0.8876, 0.9645] to 0.9408 [0.9053,
0.9763], MRR@5 ambiguous 0.6089 [0.4890, 0.7223] to 0.6301 [0.5160, 0.7394], nDCG@10 0.8392
[0.7973, 0.8772] to 0.8427 [0.8019, 0.8802]. The all pool moves the same queries: gated hit@5
0.8031 to 0.8135, ungated 0.8135 to 0.8238. Q193 (expects CLEAN-DEAD-001) moves on both arms from

CLEAN-DEAD-001 now ranks above its target; the gated arm does not see it, as Q66's signal (0.296
after) is below 0.30 and it abstains in both runs. False injection did not change: ungated 1.0000
[0.8389, 1.0000] and gated 0.2000 [0.0807, 0.4160] before and after.

### 4. Hold the cosine/max-cosine vector normalization

Measured on the ungated arm: MRR@5 ambiguous 0.6089 to 0.6674 (9 wins, 2 losses, sign test
p=0.065), hit@5 0.8135 to 0.8187, nDCG@10 0.7348 to 0.7452. Every interval overlaps its
counterpart, so by the program rule there is no decision. No code change. The ranking weights were
swept with the current normalization, so a fair test of the new one needs a weight re-sweep first;
that is the precondition for revisiting it.

## Consequences

- The served threshold stays 0.30; telemetry continues to read `abstain_signal` as the best
  surviving cosine.
- Retrieval records from this date report the eligible pool as the headline and carry the
  definitional note; older records are comparable only through the labelled all-queries pool.
- The benchmark and the measurement script share one channel partition, one mandatory read and one
  routed check, so the two can no longer disagree about membership or delivery.
- A threshold change now has a reproducible procedure (`measure`, then `sweep`) and a stated
  precondition (a larger negative set).
- Still open under 1f: the FW-M2 rule-cluster false-positive magnet and the remaining
  sibling-rule collisions.
