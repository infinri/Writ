## Reading

Every verdict is no decision, so item 1d ships as a correctness fix with no quality claim.

- Ungated: hit@5, MRR@5 and nDCG@10 are flat; false injection stays 1.00 (the ungated arm
  never abstains by construction).
- Gated (what the daemon serves): MRR@5 on the ambiguous set fell 0.6124 to 0.5663 with
  overlapping intervals and a sign test of 1 win / 4 losses (p=0.375). A few gold queries now
  abstain because Stage 1 filtering removes their top raw hit before the gate reads it. This is
  unresolved, not shown harmless.
- Gated false injection fell 0.30 to 0.20, which is 6 to 4 of only 20 negative queries, with
  no interval. Suggestive only.
- The 0.30 threshold was tuned on unfiltered hits and is not retuned here; that is open work
  for item 1f.
