# Writ community code of conduct

## Scope

This policy applies to Writ's issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly represents the project.

## Expected behavior

Treat other participants with respect. Criticize ideas and code with specific evidence rather than attacking people. Welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes and respect privacy.

Good-faith disagreement, blunt technical criticism and reporting a flaw are welcome. Participants do not need to praise Writ or agree with the maintainer.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, publishing private information without permission and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Do not retaliate against someone for making a good-faith report.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at [infinri@gmail.com](mailto:infinri@gmail.com). Include relevant links, what happened and any immediate safety or privacy concern. Do not post private details in a public issue.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will handle reports as privately as reasonably possible and disclose information only as needed to assess or address the report. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. Writ is maintained in personal time and does not offer a guaranteed response deadline.

## Enforcement and review

The maintainer may request a correction, remove content, restrict participation temporarily or permanently, or refer abuse to GitHub. Consider severity, repetition, context and the safety of participants. Explain the decision to affected participants when appropriate without exposing a reporter's private information.

An affected participant may request reconsideration by providing relevant new information through the private reporting contact. A single-maintainer project has no independent appeals body. Reports involving the maintainer can also be made through GitHub's own reporting tools.

## Responsibility

Lucio Saldivar, GitHub account [infinri](https://github.com/infinri), currently administers this policy. Future maintainers should update the reporting contact and responsibilities when they change.
